# Live family viewer

The frozen noon film and ZIP remain immutable. `demos/noon/build.py` reads completed producer evidence, exports exact native CAD views, then applies the live adapters and normalization here.

- Families are small, medium, large, original; stage is separate.
- Main medium Stage1 ends at its first full native handoff. Historical policy trials remain a separate chapter, and the actual later Stage2 seed is explicitly identified.
- Candidate and retained native counts are distinct. Diagnostic retention does not mean valid.
- Stage2 accepts only valid official scores. Geometry source and score comparator are separate; missing forecasts are disclosed.
- Original baseline arms are separate; the repaired-geometry44-open epoch never joins the raw-original curve. Uniform120 input is explicitly a preview until its producer completes routing.
- Only saved geometry is displayed; KiCad mode0 absolute coordinates are rendered with one common viewBox, grid and scale bar per family. Playback is presentation time. Chart bounds use the entire captured replay and do not refit while playing; new data waits for Follow latest.

Run `python3 demos/noon/watch.py --out <live-output>` to refresh. The publisher records immutable snapshots and downloads native evidence to verify hashes. Medium publication belongs to its producer; original and large publication belongs to this view owner.
